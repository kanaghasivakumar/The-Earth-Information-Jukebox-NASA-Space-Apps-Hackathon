    How this score was shaped

      This is an artistic reading of normalized visual signals. It does not claim latent patterns are named scientific quantities or that the audio measures physical values.

      How visuals become music

      A variational autoencoder (VAE) learns compact patterns from the visual frames. We track how those patterns shift over time, alongside image-motion cues from optical flow. The composer turns these changing signals into rhythm, dynamics, and melodic movement; each style gives the same signals a different musical character.

      The latent patterns are learned visual patterns, not physical quantities. The music is an artistic mapping, not a claim about emotion or natural sound.

      This listening lens

      Instrument names describe MIDI score choices; the built-in WAV renderer approximates those timbres. Section names describe musical structure, not scientific phases.

      A small invitation to explore data through sound. Use headphones, or leave the video paused and listen to the timeline instead.
