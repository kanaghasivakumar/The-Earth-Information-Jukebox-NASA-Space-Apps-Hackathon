## Direction contract

THESIS: One storm season, five ways to hear it. The surface owns a single synchronized instrument: the footage, the score and one shared timeline move together, and the visitor can scrub any of them. It refuses the landing-page-plus-card-grid arrangement and the glowing-starfield space cliche.

OWN-WORLD: Space Apps blue gradient ground deepening toward #151622 at the bottom. White Fira Sans in heavy weights for headings, regular for reading. Neon yellow is the only action color (play, active style, playhead). Hairline white-at-low-alpha rules, square-ish 4px corners, no glass, no glow. Region lanes use four fixed hues from the brand set with letters A-D so color is never the only cue. Fira Code only for timecodes.

STORY: A first-time visitor sees a real NASA hurricane-season globe, presses the yellow Play, hears it, and sees the timeline show which learned visual pattern is current. They try another style at the same moment and hear the same footage read differently. They leave understanding this is an artistic sonification of imagery.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar (wordmark left, Listen / How it works / Accessibility right). Below, left 55%: the square globe footage at full column width with a plain-language live caption under it. Right 45%: heading "Hear a hurricane season." with two lines of explanation, the five style choices as a single list with one active, then the transport row with a large yellow Play. The shared timeline spans the full width beneath both. Play sits within the first 700px.

FORM: Synchronized player with audio as master clock, video slaved; seed: none (unavailable). Position 1 of 1; pinned.
